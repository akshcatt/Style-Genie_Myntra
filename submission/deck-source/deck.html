Open Project 2026 · Myntra StyleGenie · 01 · Problem, Impact & Users

Fashion e-commerce is broken at the moment of decision

An AI-native styling layer on Myntra. Core insight: people don't want to shop for clothes — they want to look good for a moment.

🌀 Choice paralysis

9 lakh+ products. Users browse 40+ items per purchase. More options → harder decisions → ~70% cart abandonment.

📏 Fit uncertainty

The #1 driver of returns. 30–40% of fashion is returned because static size charts ignore brand variation, fabric & body diversity.

🧠 No contextual intelligence

Keyword search can't understand "something for a college fest under 2K" — an occasion, a budget and a vibe in one sentence.

🔁 Labour-intensive discovery

Users are trapped in a browse → filter → scroll loop. No way to say "like what that influencer wore, in my budget".

Who it hits: the occasion shopper, the overwhelmed browser, the serial returner, the trend seeker. Impact & hypothesis: these directly cost revenue via lost conversion, costly reverse logistics & low engagement. Hypothesis — layering GenAI on the existing flow lifts conversion, cuts returns and raises order value without a re-platform.

02 · Why GenAI (not predictive ML)

These capabilities are categorically impossible with traditional ML

Traditional approachWhy it falls shortWhat GenAI unlocks
Collaborative filteringBackward-looking — "people who bought X bought Y". Can't grasp a new intent.LLM parses intent: occasion + budget + vibe + reference, all at once.
Keyword searchMatches tokens, not meaning. "red dress" yes; "Alia's look but under 3K" no.NLU + reasoning over nuanced, multi-constraint natural language.
Static size chartsLookup tables. Blind to "this brand runs slim in the shoulders".LLM mines 1000s of unstructured reviews into a fit signal.
Rule-based recosSuggests items; can't compose a coordinated, occasion-right outfit.Generative composition — head-to-toe looks with creative judgement.
Manual catalog taggingCan't read a Pinterest screenshot's aesthetic.Multimodal (CLIP) maps image → catalog in a shared embedding space.

The unlock is the orchestration layer: LLMs provide intelligence (understanding & generation); a RAG pipeline over the live catalog provides facts (what Myntra actually sells). Intelligence + grounding = a trustworthy AI system, not a chatbot.

03 · Users & Solution Overview

Who we serve & the high-level solution

🎯 Target users

  • The Occasion Shopper — "I have an event in 3 days, dress me."
  • The Overwhelmed Browser — adds to cart, abandons from choice fatigue.
  • The Returner — buys 3, keeps 1; burned by fit.
  • The Trend Seeker — Gen-Z, shops inspiration & references, not SKUs.

💡 Core insight

Users think in occasions, aesthetics & references — not categories & keywords. The UI should meet them there.

🧞 The solution: StyleGenie

A GenAI layer dropped on top of the existing Myntra app (same UI, AI woven in) with five features spanning the journey:

  • StyleGenie Chat — describe it, get curated looks
  • Visual Match — snap any image → shop it
  • Outfit Studio — generate complete coordinated looks
  • FitGenius — your true size, with evidence
  • TrendPulse — a daily, personal AI lookbook

Intent → discovery → confidence → checkout, each step de-frictioned.

06 · Pitfalls & Workarounds

We designed for the hard parts

RiskConcernMitigation
🚫 HallucinationsAI recommends products that don't exist.RAG grounding — every response retrieved from the live catalog; the LLM never invents SKUs. Confidence scoring filters low-certainty output.
🔒 PrivacyUsers wary of sharing photos & body data.On-device photo processing, explicit opt-in, transparent data policy, zero third-party sharing.
⚡ LatencyAI too slow for a shopping flow.Streaming responses, pre-computed embedding caches, edge-deployed CLIP. Target <2s.
🤝 User trustUsers don't trust AI styling advice.Show confidence + review evidence; manual override at every step; progressive trust-building.
📈 Scalability / costLLM cost explodes at 50M+ MAU.Tiered models (cheap for simple, premium for complex), aggressive caching, batch for non-realtime.
👀 AdoptionUsers ignore AI features.Soft inline integration, contextual nudges on struggle signals, free tier + premium upsell.

The bottom line: StyleGenie isn't a bolt-on chatbot — it's an intent-first, grounded, measurable re-imagining of fashion discovery that reduces user effort and moves the numbers that matter to Myntra.
